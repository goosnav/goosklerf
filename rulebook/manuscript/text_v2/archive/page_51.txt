SCENARIO C: THE MEAL

FLAVOR

You awake and all around you is white. Not a room. Not fog. Not
snow. Just white, in every direction, with no floor and no ceiling
and no walls, and you standing in the middle of it like a thought
someone hasn't finished thinking yet.

Then the crack.

It starts small, a hairline fracture in the white, and then the
white splits apart and you are falling. You fall for what feels
like hours. You fall long enough to get bored of falling. You fall
long enough to start wondering if falling is just what you do now.

Then you hit something hard and hot and everything changes.

The heat hits your face first. A blinding, searing, greasy heat
that smells like breakfast. You push yourself up and your hands
press into something slick and warm and yellow. You are standing
in a yolk. A yolk the size of a parking lot, trembling slightly,
stretched out across a frying pan that extends in every direction
to a horizon that curves upward like the lip of a bowl.

Around you, others are climbing to their feet. They came from the
same egg. They look as confused as you feel. The white of the egg
stretches out behind you like a rubber desert. The pan beneath it
all is black and enormous and radiating heat from below in waves
that make the air shimmer.

In the distance, past the yolk, past the white, past the curved
edge of the pan, you can see something: a timer. A kitchen timer,
the size of a building, ticking. You cannot read the numbers from
here but you can hear it. Every tick vibrates through the pan and
up through your feet.

When the timer reaches zero, whoever set it is coming back. And
they are hungry. And you are breakfast.

Unless you are not.

The goal is simple. Make a meal before you become one. If you can
prepare something worth eating before the timer runs out, whatever
enormous thing is coming back to eat you will eat that instead. If
you cannot, you will be served sunny-side up on a plate you will
never see the edges of, and that will be the end of you.

SETUP

Each character begins with:
    -   Their base entity card (character sheet)
    -   0 items
    -   HP at maximum

SPECIAL SETUP - THE MEAL PLAN. Before the scenario begins, the
party chooses a difficulty by deciding how many courses they will
attempt to prepare. More courses means a bigger meal, which means
a more satisfied creature, which means a better chance of survival.
It also means more fighting, more scavenging, and less time.

    1-COURSE MEAL (EASY)
        Ingredients needed: 3 entities + 3 items argued as edible
        Serviceware needed: 1 item argued as a utensil, cup, OR plate
        Time: 16 rounds total across the scenario

    2-COURSE MEAL (MEDIUM)
        Ingredients needed: 6 entities + 6 items argued as edible
        Serviceware needed: 2 of the following (no duplicates):
                            utensil, cup, plate
        Time: 12 rounds total across the scenario

    3-COURSE MEAL (HARD)
        Ingredients needed: 9 entities + 9 items argued as edible
        Serviceware needed: all 3: utensil AND cup AND plate
        Time: 10 rounds total across the scenario

    4-COURSE MEAL (IMPOSSIBLE)
        Ingredients needed: 12 entities + 12 items argued as edible
        Serviceware needed: all 3: utensil AND cup AND plate
        Time: 8 rounds total across the scenario

Each course requires exactly 3 defeated entities and 3 items
argued as edible, plus the party must collectively explain what
dish these 6 components make and how they cooked it.

The DM tracks the round timer. Every combat round and every
non-combat action round counts toward the timer. When the timer
reaches zero, the scenario ends, regardless of progress. If the
meal is incomplete, the party loses.
