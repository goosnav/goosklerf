QUICK REFERENCE CARD

(Cut out and keep at the table)


TURN PHASES (IN ORDER)
    1. CARD PLAY       Play 3 cards from hand, or discard to reach 3 total
    2. COMBAT          Declare engagements; resolve each fully
    3. MOVEMENT        Reposition entities and transfer your own items
    4. CARD DRAW       Draw 2 random + search 1; reshuffle
    5. VICTORY CHECK   Check win conditions and Landlord status


HOW TO HIT
    Roll 1d6. If the result is equal to or below Attack: HIT.
    A normal hit deals 1 HP damage unless a card says otherwise.


COMBAT ACTIONS
    Basic:      Normal Attack / Use Consumable / Use Special Ability
    Advanced:   Parry / Retreat / Item Swap / Heal / Supercharged Attack
                Buy an Extra Action


FORTRESS ASSAULT
    Attackers may target the fortress or defenders inside it.
    If all defenders are gone and the fortress has 1+ HP, the attacker may:
        CAPTURE: move at least 1 surviving attacker inside; control transfers.
        DESTROY: discard the fortress; attackers remain on battlefield.

    If the fortress reaches 0 HP, it is destroyed with everything inside.


RETREAT
    Announce retreat instead of acting. Survive the next enemy round.
    Then leave to a legal destination. No legal destination = no retreat.


SHOP
    Buy 1 shop card by discarding 2 normal cards OR 1 rare card.
    Bought card must be played immediately and counts as 1 of 3 plays.


VICTORY CONDITIONS
    LAST MAN STANDING   Only your entities survive. Win immediately.
    LANDLORD            Control all fortresses, no unplayed fortresses anywhere,
                        then survive one full round.
    HAMLET              All entities gone, none can enter play. Everyone loses.
    SPECIAL             Card or scenario effect declares a winner.


STAT LIMITS
    Attack: 1-5        HP per die: 0-6        Items per entity: 0-3
    Entities per fortress: 0-3                Battlefield entities: 0-5 per player


VICTORY REWARDS
    Each enemy entity defeated: +1 card
    Each fortress captured:     +2 cards
    Each fortress destroyed:    +3 cards
