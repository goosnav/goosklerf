VISUAL ELEMENTS CHECKLIST (FOR THE ARTIST)

The following illustrations are referenced or implied throughout
this rulebook. Each is listed with its purpose and the minimum
information it must communicate. Illustrations should be
functional first, stylish second. A player should be able to
understand the thing from the illustration alone.

FIGURE 1 - CARD ANATOMY
    One full page. Four card diagrams side by side (Entity,
    Fortress, Item, Consumable). Callouts label: border color,
    name, card-type icon, Attack stat, HP dice (note: normal
    entities have 1 die, super entities 2; normal fortresses 2
    dice, super fortresses 3), capacity (if any), text box, and
    any buff/debuff indicators.
    Purpose: So a new player can identify every part of a card
    by glancing at the page.

FIGURE 2 - TABLE LAYOUT (MOST IMPORTANT IMAGE IN THE BOOK)
    One full page. Top-down view of a 2-player Classic Mode
    table. Clearly show: battlefield in the center, suburbs
    above and below with slots for up to 3 fortresses per
    player, each player's deck/hand/graveyard near them, optional
    shop column on one side. Label every zone. Use simple boxes,
    not decoration.

FIGURE 3 - TURN FLOW DIAGRAM
    Half page. A flow diagram of the five phases (Card Play,
    Combat, Movement, Card Draw, Victory Check) with a one-line
    summary of each.

FIGURE 4 - COMBAT WALKTHROUGH
    Full page. A comic-style walkthrough of one complete
    engagement: declaration, initial volley, two combat rounds,
    resolution, and victory reward. Use real numbers (dice
    rolls shown). This is the image new players will point at
    when they are stuck.

FIGURE 5 - FORTRESS ASSAULT VS BARRAGE
    Half page. Two small diagrams side by side. Left: entities
    moving from the battlefield to assault a fortress. Right:
    fortress entities firing across the suburbs at another
    fortress ("ships fighting ships"). Shows the visual
    difference between the two combat types.

FIGURE 6 - RULE LEVELS CHART
    Half page. A simple chart mapping Basic Goosklerf, Advanced
    Goosklerf, and Custom Goosklerf. Basic uses Core Rules only.
    Advanced uses all Advanced Rules. Custom uses whichever
    Advanced Rules players agree to before setup.

FIGURE 7 - MODE SELECTOR
    Half page. A small grid of the five modes with a one-line
    description, recommended player count, and difficulty
    rating.

FIGURE 8 - TACTICAL MODE LAYOUTS
    Half page. Three small table layouts for Lightning,
    Standard Tactical, and Skirmish variants.
