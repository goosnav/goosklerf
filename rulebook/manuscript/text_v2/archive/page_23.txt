14. VICTORY REWARDS (FIRST STRIKE INCENTIVE)

Aggression is rewarded. When an engagement ends in attacker
victory, the attacker draws cards from the top of their deck based
on what was accomplished during the engagement. Rewards stack per
outcome:

        Each enemy entity defeated:    +1 card
        Each fortress captured:        +2 cards
        Each fortress destroyed:       +3 cards

Total reward = sum of the above. There is no cap. Drawn cards are
not playable this turn; they become available on the attacker's
next turn like any other hand cards.

EXCEPTIONS. Defenders receive no Victory Reward. No reward is
granted for mutual-kill outcomes, Initial Volleys, or Fortress
Barrages (Section 25). If the attacker's deck is empty, no cards
are drawn. The reward exists specifically to make declaring
battlefield combat a better choice than sitting still.

TIMING. Victory Rewards are drawn during Combat Resolution, after
all captures, destruction, retreats, Last Stand actions, and
Scavenger effects from that engagement have resolved.

15. OLD AGE

At the end of a player's turn, if that player has gone 3 of their
own turns without declaring an engagement, randomly choose one of
their surviving entities. That entity dies and is discarded.

Declaring any engagement resets the count. Initial Volley does not
reset the count. Fortress Barrage does.

Old Age cannot be prevented, healed, revived, or used for Last Stand.

If a player's last entity dies from Old Age, that player loses.
