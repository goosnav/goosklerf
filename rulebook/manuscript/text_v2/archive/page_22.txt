PART III - ADVANCED RULES

Advanced Rules expand the Core Rules.

In Basic Goosklerf, ignore this part.

In Advanced Goosklerf, use every rule in this part.

In Custom Goosklerf, players choose which Advanced Rules are active
before setup.

A rules-selection sheet is provided in the appendix for Custom
Goosklerf.

A. ECONOMY AND GAME PRESSURE

13. SHOP

At the start of the game, each player draws cards at random from
their deck and places them face-up in their own shop area. The
number of shop cards depends on the deck regime (see Section 8):

        Small Deck:    3 shop cards
        Medium Deck:   7 shop cards
        Large Deck:    7 shop cards

Shop cards belong to the player who placed them. Only that player
may buy from their own shop. All shop cards are always visible to
all players throughout the game.

BUYING. During the active player's Card Play phase, they may
purchase one or more shop cards. To buy 1 shop card, the active
player must do one of the following:

    -   Discard 2 normal cards from their hand, OR
    -   Discard 1 rare card from their hand (rare = purple entity,
        orange fortress, or yellow/gold item).

A purchased card must be played or equipped immediately using the
normal placement rules. If it cannot be legally placed, it cannot
be purchased. Each shop purchase counts as one of the active
player's 3 played cards for the turn.

The shop does not refill on its own. Once a shop card is bought,
that slot is gone for the rest of the game unless a special rule
refills it.
