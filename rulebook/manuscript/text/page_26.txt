21. HEALING

Entities have the choice to heal themselves or another friendly
entity in the same engagement. To heal, the acting entity rolls for
an attack (1d6 against its modified Attack stat). On a hit, the
healer randomly draws a card from their own deck and discards it.
This heals any single friendly entity in the engagement by +1 HP.
On a miss, the entity's action is consumed and no card is discarded.

Healing cannot raise an entity's HP above its printed maximum unless
a card explicitly says so.


22. EXTRA ACTIONS

In combat, entities use only ONE action per round by default. An
entity may buy one additional action by drawing from its stamina
reserves: randomly draw 3 cards from the entity's controlling
player's deck and discard them. This grants the entity one
additional action this round.

There is no limit on Extra Actions purchased per round per entity,
other than deck depth. The cost stacks: two extra actions = 6
discarded cards. The extra action may be any normal action,
including Heal, Reinforcement call, Supercharged Attack, or special
ability.


23. VICTORY REWARDS (FIRST STRIKE INCENTIVE)

Aggression is rewarded. When an engagement ends in attacker
victory, the attacker draws cards from the top of their deck based
on what was accomplished during the engagement. Rewards stack per
outcome:

    Each enemy entity defeated:    +1 card
    Each fortress captured:        +2 cards
    Each fortress destroyed:       +3 cards

Total reward = sum of the above. There is no cap. Drawn cards are
not playable this turn; they become available on the attacker's
next turn like any other hand cards.

Defenders receive no Victory Reward. Mutual-kill engagements (both
sides wiped out simultaneously) grant no reward to either side. If
the attacker's deck is empty, no cards are drawn.

Victory Rewards are NOT triggered by Initial Volleys, by Fortress
Barrages (Section 20), or by mutual-kill outcomes. The reward
exists specifically to make declaring battlefield combat a better
choice than sitting still.

TIMING. Victory Rewards are drawn during Combat Resolution, after
all captures, destruction, retreats, Last Stand actions, and
Scavenger effects from that engagement have resolved.
