SCENARIO C - ACT IV: THE FINALE

Objective: Destroy or seize the distortion machine.

The Core Lab is the super fortress set aside at setup.

Inside it is the machine. The machine is large and quiet. It
does not look like science. It looks like furniture, the kind
of furniture you find in a room that has been waiting for
someone for a very long time. There is a chair in front of
it. The chair has restraints. The restraints are open.

Standing next to the machine is the boss: Attack 5, HP 8, and
a consumable that rolls as a 2-damage attack on all attackers
in the engagement. The boss is wearing a lab coat. The boss's
wrist bracelet has the name of one of the party members on it.

THE BRACELET REVELATION. Before the fight begins, the DM
should tell the players what they have begun to suspect: each
character is wearing a bracelet with the name of one of the
others. The boss is wearing the name of one specific party
member: the DM picks. That party member is the only one in
the party whose bracelet does NOT have someone in the party on
it. Their bracelet has the boss's name. They have always had
it. They just have not read it carefully.

The implication is left for the party to interpret. The DM
should not explain it.

The party may:
    - Destroy the machine. Defeat the boss in combat, then
      assault the fortress (the Core Lab) until it reaches
      0 HP. This ends the scenario: the lab collapses, the
      party escapes, and the distortions in this district end.
      The bracelets crumble to dust as the party leaves.
    - Seize the machine. Defeat the boss, but leave the
      fortress standing. The first character to touch the
      machine after the boss dies gains +1 Attack permanently
      but is now connected to it. At the start of every future
      scenario, that character must roll 1d6. On a 1, they
      glitch: lose 1 HP permanently and lose 1 turn at the
      start of the next combat.
    - Sit in the chair. One character may volunteer to sit in
      the chair after the boss is defeated. The chair will not
      let them up. The machine will start doing whatever it
      does. The character is gone at the end of the scenario,
      but the rest of the party walks out clean with no
      penalty, and one specific thing about the world is
      restored: the DM picks something small and personal to
      one of the remaining party members.


AFTERMATH

Escape from the lab is automatic for all surviving characters.
The corridors that took hours to traverse on the way in take
minutes on the way out. The doors are unlocked. The fluorescent
lights have begun to fail one by one as the party walks out.
The voice on the intercom has gone silent.

Outside, the sun is rising. None of the party can quite agree
on whether the sun was rising when they went in. None of them
can quite agree on how long it has been.

The character who sat in the chair, if any, is not with the
party when they reach the parking lot. None of the others can
quite remember them anymore. Their items are still in the
party's packs. Their name is still on someone's bracelet.
That bracelet does not crumble.

You may or may not find out who you were before this started.
You probably won't. But you know who the others are now, and
you know they came back for you, and that is more than most
people in this world get to keep.


[VISUAL NOTE: The Core Lab interior: a machine that looks like furniture in a waiting room, a chair with open restraints, one figure in a lab coat with a bracelet. The machine is quiet and wrong. Below or beside: the party walking out of the lab as lights fail behind them, one fewer figure than there were going in. Understated and melancholy.]
