9. COMBAT

Combat begins when the active player declares an engagement. The
player declaring combat is the attacker. All opposing entities
involved are the defenders.


WHO CAN ATTACK WHOM

    Entities on the battlefield may attack:
        - Enemy battlefield entities
        - Enemy fortresses in the suburbs (fortress assault)

    Fortress entities may attack:
        - Enemy entities on the battlefield, but only once per
          engagement, as an Initial Volley first strike (see below)
        - Enemies attacking their fortress, during that assault
        - Enemy fortresses, only if the Fortress Barrage rule is
          active (see Section 20)


INITIAL VOLLEY (FORTRESS ENTITIES)

When a battlefield engagement is declared, any fortress with
surviving occupants may fire a single Initial Volley into that
engagement, unless that fortress is currently being assaulted
or participating in a Fortress Barrage. Both attackers and
defenders may fire an Initial Volley before combat begins.

Key limits:

    ONE VOLLEY PER FORTRESS PER ENGAGEMENT. A fortress may
    contribute at most one volley shot per battlefield
    engagement, regardless of how many entities are inside
    it. The player controlling the fortress picks which
    entity occupant fires.

    NO VOLLEYS DURING FORTRESS ATTACKS. A fortress that is
    currently under assault, or participating in a Fortress
    Barrage, cannot fire a volley. Its occupants are already
    committed to the fight at their door.

    RESETS EACH ENGAGEMENT. The volley counter resets at the
    start of each new engagement. A fortress that volleyed
    in the previous engagement may volley again in the next.

The volley shot is a single normal attack against any one enemy
entity participating in the battlefield engagement, chosen by
the volleying player. Volleys cannot target fortresses. The
volley resolves before the first combat round begins.
