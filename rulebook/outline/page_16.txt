POSITIONING MATTERS

Where entities end the Movement phase directly determines what
actions are possible on the following turn.

    - Only entities on the battlefield can initiate fortress
      assaults. Entities inside fortresses cannot capture enemy
      fortresses.

    - A fortress with no defending entities can be captured
      immediately when assaulted, with no resistance.

    - If a player does not have entities on the battlefield by their
      next combat turn, they cannot initiate any fortress captures
      during their combat turn (since fortresses can only be invaded
      from the battlefield).

    - Entities left on the battlefield are exposed to attack.
      Entities inside fortresses are safer but cannot project
      force outward to capture territory.

Players must balance protection and projection: Too many entities in
fortresses limits offensive capability; too many on the battlefield
risks losing control of defended positions. Poor positioning at the
end of Movement will often decide the next turn before it begins.

Items may be freely transferred between the player's own entities
during this phase, as long as capacity limits are respected.
Fortresses cannot hold items. If an item must move somewhere, it
must ride on an entity.

Once Movement is complete, no further repositioning may happen
until the active player's next turn. (Combat-related staging
movements only occur during Phase 2 and are not affected by this
lock.)


PHASE 4: CARD DRAW

The active player draws the top 2 cards of their deck at random.
They then look through the entire deck and select 1 card
deliberately. All 3 cards are added to their hand. If it is
possible to draw 3 cards, the active player must draw 3 (or the
maximum number possible). The deck must then be thoroughly
reshuffled. In competitive play, the player to the active
player's left must shuffle the deck and return it to the active player.


PHASE 5: VICTORY CHECK

At the end of the turn, after Card Draw is complete, check
whether any victory condition has been met (Section 6). If so,
the game ends. Landlord status is checked and tracked here.
During the victory check, players must communicate whether or not
they have any unplayed fortresses in their hand or deck. Players
may look through their own deck to confirm. Each time a player
looks through their deck, they must shuffle thoroughly a minimum
of 3 times.
