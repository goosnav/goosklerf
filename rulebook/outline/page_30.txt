26. TACTICAL MODE

Tactical Mode is a fixed-loadout battle with no hand refill. Every
card a player gets is what they start with. 2 to 4 players.

Tactical Mode comes in three deck-size variants. Players must
choose one before starting.


26.1 LIGHTNING (FAST)

Each player gets:
    - 3 entities
    - 1 fortress
    - 7 items (distributed as desired, up to 1 per entity to start)

No deck, no hand. At setup, each player places their cards as
desired on the battlespace. The fortress goes in the player's
suburbs and entities are placed either on the battlefield or in
the fortress.

Each round, players take turns performing Combat and Movement
phases only. No Card Play, no Card Draw, and no refill.

Recommended rules: Core only. Retreat and Parry optional.


26.2 STANDARD TACTICAL

Each player gets:
    - 5 entities
    - 1 fortress
    - 11 items selected before the game begins
    - 7-item reserve hand (the items not yet equipped)

At setup, each player places their fortress in their suburbs
and their entities on the battlefield or in their fortress.
Each entity may begin with up to 2 equipped items.

Turn structure is Combat, Movement, then an Equipment Phase
where the active player may equip any items from their reserve
hand to their entities, respecting capacity. No deck is used.

Recommended rules: All advanced rules enabled.
