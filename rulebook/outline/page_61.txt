VISUAL ELEMENTS CHECKLIST (FOR THE ARTIST)


The following illustrations are referenced throughout this rulebook.
Each is listed with its purpose and the minimum information it must
communicate. Illustrations should be functional first, stylish second.
A player should be able to understand the thing from the illustration
alone.


FIGURE 1 - CARD ANATOMY (page 08)
    Four card diagrams side by side: Entity, Fortress, Item, Consumable.
    Callouts label: border color, name, card-type icon, Attack stat,
    HP dice (normal entity = 1 die, super entity = 2; normal fortress
    = 2 dice, super fortress = 3), capacity, text box, buff/debuff.

FIGURE 2 - TABLE LAYOUT (page 09) [MOST IMPORTANT]
    Top-down 2-player table: battlefield center, suburbs top and bottom,
    each player's deck/hand/graveyard, optional shop. Label every zone.

FIGURE 3 - TURN FLOW DIAGRAM (page 14)
    Five boxes in sequence: CARD PLAY / COMBAT / MOVEMENT / CARD DRAW /
    VICTORY CHECK. One-line summary below each box.

FIGURE 4 - COMBAT WALKTHROUGH (page 18)
    Comic-style walkthrough: declaration, initial volley, 2 combat rounds,
    resolution, victory reward. Show real dice numbers.

FIGURE 5 - FORTRESS ASSAULT VS BARRAGE (page 20)
    Left: entities assaulting a fortress from battlefield.
    Right: two fortresses firing at each other (ship to ship).

FIGURE 6 - RULE LEVELS CHART (page 22)
    Grid: which rules are on/off for Core Only / Core + Shop / Full Advanced.

FIGURE 7 - MODE SELECTOR (page 28)
    Grid: mode name, player count, description, difficulty for all 5 modes.

FIGURE 8 - TACTICAL MODE LAYOUTS (page 31)
    Three small table layouts: Lightning / Standard Tactical / Skirmish.

FIGURE 9 - SINGLEPLAYER BATTLEPATH EXAMPLE (page 32)
    6-level battlepath with fortress counts, enemy counts, player strike
    force, and shop.

FIGURE 10 - CAMPAIGN CHARACTER SHEET (page 38)
    Blank printable sheet: name, HP, Attack, items, consumables,
    level, rerolls, notes.

FIGURE 11 - CHESSKLERF BOARD SETUP (page 40)
    8x8 board: corner fortresses (a1, h1, a8, h8), major pieces on
    back rank, pawns on ranks 4 and 5 facing each other.

FIGURE 12 - QUICK REFERENCE CARD (page 62)
    Turn summary, hit rule, action list, capture vs destroy, retreat,
    shop, victory conditions. No flavor text. Pure reference. Tear-out.

GENERAL ART NOTES
    Setting: post-reality-collapse grimdark with absurd undertones.
    Visual flavors: broken geometry, wrong animals in wrong places,
    humans who are almost human, concrete bureaucracy that has
    outlived its purpose, fluorescent light. Keep rulebook interior
    art clean and functional; save heavy mood for scenario chapter
    headers, card art, and the setting page.
