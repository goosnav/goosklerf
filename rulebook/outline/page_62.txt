QUICK REFERENCE CARD

(Cut out and keep at the table)


TURN PHASES (IN ORDER)
    1. CARD PLAY    Play 3 cards from hand (or discard to reach 3 total)
    2. COMBAT       Declare any number of engagements; resolve each fully
    3. MOVEMENT     Reposition entities and transfer items freely
    4. CARD DRAW    Draw 2 random + pick 1 deliberately; reshuffle deck
    5. VICTORY CHECK  Check win conditions; track Landlord status


HOW TO HIT
    Roll 1d6. If result is equal to or below your Attack value: HIT (1 HP damage).
    Above your Attack value: MISS.


COMBAT ACTIONS (EACH ENTITY GETS 1 PER ROUND)
    A. Normal attack       D. Parry (optional rule)
    B. Use a consumable    E. Retreat (optional rule)
    C. Use a special       F. Item Swap (optional rule)
                           G. Heal (optional rule)


CAPTURE vs DESTROY (AFTER FORTRESS ASSAULT)
    CAPTURE: Garrison at least 1 entity. Control transfers to attacker.
    DESTROY: Discard fortress. All inside destroyed. Attacker stays on battlefield.


RETREAT
    Announce retreat. Take no action this round.
    Survive one round of enemy attacks. Then leave to a legal destination.
    No destination = cannot retreat.


SHOP (optional rule)
    Buy 1 shop card by discarding: 2 normal cards OR 1 rare card.
    Bought card must be played immediately. Counts as 1 of your 3 played cards.


VICTORY CONDITIONS
    LAST MAN STANDING    Only your entities survive. Win immediately.
    LANDLORD             Control all fortresses, no unplayed fortresses anywhere.
                         Survive one full round. Win.
    HAMLET               All entities gone, none can enter play. Everyone loses.
    SPECIAL              Card or scenario effect declares a winner.


STAT LIMITS
    Attack: 1-5     HP per die: 0-6     Items per entity: 0-3
    Entities per fortress: 0-3          Battlefield entities: 0-5 per player


VICTORY REWARDS (optional rule)
    Each enemy entity defeated: +1 card
    Each fortress captured:     +2 cards
    Each fortress destroyed:    +3 cards
